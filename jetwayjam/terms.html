Some things in the game are bought with real money through the App Store: coin packs, chests, Airlift and Head Start packs, bundles and offers, the Piggy Bank, the Flyer Card, the Golden Pass, the VIP Lounge, and the No Ads subscription. Advertising

The game shows advertisements, but not at first: none at all during your first ten minutes of play. After that, one full-screen advertisement at the end of every fifth game you finish, and short videos you may choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. The No Ads subscription stops the ones you did not ask for; the optional ones remain available if you want them.

The No Ads subscription

No Ads is an auto-renewing subscription of one month, priced at $7.99 per month (or the equivalent price shown in the game for your country). It removes the full-screen advertisements between levels for as long as it is active, and it changes nothing else: no level, skin or reward is locked behind it.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically each month unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage the subscription and turn off auto-renew in your Apple ID account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) at any time after purchase. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the period you have already paid for runs to its end.

If the subscription lapses, the advertisements return. Nothing else about your game changes.
