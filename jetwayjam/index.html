Jetway Jam

Airport Jam Puzzle — Support

An airport jam puzzle: lead a crowd of travellers through the terminal, then tap the planes out of the jam so every passenger boards a plane of their colour. 300 levels in 12 chapters.

How do I play?

Every level has two parts. In the terminal, drag left and right to steer your group: number signs add or multiply travellers, airport hazards cost you some, and you need at least as many passengers as the planes have seats. On the apron, tap a plane to taxi it out of the jam to a free gate; passengers of the same colour board it and it takes off.

Are there ads?

Not for your first ten minutes of play — and nothing is for sale in that time either. After that, one ad plays at the end of every fifth game you finish, never in the middle of a level. Every other video is optional and gives you something (a free Airlift, double coins, a continue…). The No Ads subscription switches off the ads between levels.

How much is No Ads, and how do I cancel?

$7.99 per month (or the local price shown in the game), renewing automatically until you cancel. Cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, at least 24 hours before the period ends. It is managed by Apple.

I bought something and it did not arrive / I have a new phone

Open Settings in the game (the gear) or the bottom of the Store and tap Restore purchases. That brings back the Golden Pass, the VIP Lounge and an active No Ads subscription on any device signed in to the same Apple ID. Coins, chests and packs are consumable, so Apple cannot restore them — email us with the date of the purchase and we will help.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on your device only — there is no account and nothing is synced to a server. Deleting the app erases it permanently.

Where is the Store?

The Store, the Golden Pass and the No Ads button appear after your first ten minutes of play.
