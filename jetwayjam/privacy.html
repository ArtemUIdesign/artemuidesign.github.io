Jetway Jam

Airport Jam Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

The short version. Jetway Jam has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. Your levels, coins, skins, chests and settings stay on your device. The game shows ads, and the advertising is run by Google, who do collect data — that is the one part of this page worth reading properly. Purchases are handled by Apple.

What the game stores

All of this is kept locally, in the app's own storage on your device, and never sent to us:

  • your progress: the level you are on, the furthest level reached, coins, Airlifts and Head Starts
  • the plane and runner skins you own and which ones you wear
  • your Chest Vault, missions, awards, Lucky Wheel spins, Duty-Free Lounge, daily reward streak and Golden Pass tiers
  • which purchases have already been delivered (the App Store transaction ids), whether you own the Golden Pass or the VIP Lounge, and until when No Ads is active
  • how many games you have finished and how long you have played, so the game knows when an ad break is due and when your ad-free first ten minutes are over
  • your sound and vibration settings

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no cloud copy.

Ads

Your first ten minutes of play show no ads at all, and nothing is for sale during them. It is ten minutes of actual play, counted once, and it does not come back afterwards.

Ads in Jetway Jam are served by Google AdMob. There are two kinds, and neither appears in the middle of a level:

  • Rewarded videos that you choose to watch — for example to open a locked gate, for the one continue per level, to double your coins, for a free Airlift or Head Start, for free coins, or to speed up a chest. You are never shown one without tapping for it.
  • Interstitials between levels: one at the end of every fifth game you finish (won, lost or retried).

To choose and measure those ads, Google may use your device's advertising identifier together with data such as your approximate location (from the IP address), device type and how you interacted with the ad. That data goes to Google, not to us — we never see it and have no access to it. Google explains what they do with it here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. The ad software starts when the app opens and may prepare ads in the background, even though none is shown during the first ten minutes.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any personalised ad is shown, and the game's Settings (the gear) then has an Ad privacy choices button to change it at any time. On every iPhone, iOS asks whether Jetway Jam may track you across other companies' apps and websites. Saying no changes nothing about the game: you keep every feature and every reward — the ads are simply less personalised. You can change your mind at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

The No Ads subscription turns the interstitials off for as long as it is active. Rewarded videos stay available either way, because they are optional.

Purchases and the subscription

Coin packs, chests, boosters, bundles, the Flyer Card, the Golden Pass, the VIP Lounge and the No Ads subscription are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your card details, your name or your Apple ID. To deliver what you bought and to know whether No Ads is still active, the game reads the App Store transaction information on your device (product, purchase and expiry dates, transaction id) and keeps the result there. None of it leaves your device.

No Ads costs $7.99 per month (or the local equivalent shown in the game) and renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Analytics

Jetway Jam has no analytics of its own. We do not send ourselves events, sessions or crash reports.

Children

Jetway Jam is a game for a general audience. Because it shows third-party advertising it is not in the App Store's Kids category and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and we will help you have it deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published at this same address.
