Ringabout

Privacy Policy

Keep the ring moving, keep the cars apart.

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy explains how Sofia Aleksandra Subina (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile game Ringabout (the “Game”). The short version: we collect nothing about you and hold no data of yours anywhere. The Game shows advertising and can sell you things, and those two features involve Google and Apple — which is what this policy is mostly about.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not ask for your name, your email, your location, your contacts or your photos. We have no database with anything of yours in it, and we cannot identify you.

What the Game stores, and where

Your progress — levels, scores, coins, what you have unlocked, your settings — is saved on your own device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves the device. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else. Deleting the Game deletes it.

Advertising

Not at the start. A new installation shows no advertising at all for the first 10 minutes you spend playing: no full-screen ad, no consent form and no tracking request, because the advertising software is not started until then. Nothing about your device is shared with anyone during it.

After that the Game shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad every 15th game you finish, and videos you can choose to watch in exchange for something in the Game (free chips in the shop, and a second chance). There is no banner — this Game does not show one anywhere.

To show an ad, Google receives your device's advertising identifier and ordinary technical information about the device: its model, operating system version, rough region, and the fact that an ad was shown. That identifier is a number your phone gives out for advertising and nothing else; it is not your name, and you can reset it at any time in your phone's settings.

What Google is allowed to do with it depends on your answer, not on ours:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form appears the first time you play. If you refuse, the ads you see are not personalised.
  • On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks separately whether the Game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no — or never answer — the advertising identifier is not used for that.
  • You can change the tracking answer later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Refusing changes nothing else about the Game: the same levels, the same coins, the same number of ads.

Google's own explanation of what it does with advertising data is at business.safety.google/privacy.

The one way to remove the advertising entirely is the No ads subscription below. Buying it also stops the advertising identifier being shared, because no ad is requested any more.

Purchases

The Game sells a monthly No ads subscription and packs of chips. We never see your card, your address or your name: the payment happens entirely inside the App Store, under the account you already have there, and no third party of ours is involved in it at all. The Game asks the App Store what you own and the App Store answers — that is the whole of it.

Coins and everything you buy with them live on your device like the rest of your progress. A subscription lives on your App Store account and comes back with “Restore purchase”.

Sharing

The Game has a Share button. When you tap it, your phone's own share sheet opens and you choose where to send a picture of your result. What you send and to whom is between you and the app you choose; we never see it and we do not receive a copy.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and is rated accordingly. The advertising in it is restricted to a general audience (AdMob content rating “G”), and the Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect anything from a child, and the Game asks nobody for any personal detail at all. A parent who would rather their child saw no advertising can buy the No ads subscription or turn off personalised advertising in the phone's settings.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct or delete personal data exist wherever personal data does. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to give you a copy of or to delete on request. The data on your device is yours: uninstalling the Game removes it.

The store

Downloading the Game from the App Store is a transaction between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change will be announced in the store listing.
