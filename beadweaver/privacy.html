Kinetic Bead Weaver

Privacy Policy

Unweave the beads, top first.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how Sofia Aleksandra Subina (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile game Kinetic Bead Weaver (the “Game”). The short version: the Game itself collects nothing about you and we hold no data of yours anywhere. The Game shows advertising and can sell you things, and those two features involve Google and Apple — which is what this policy is mostly about.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not ask for your name, your email, your location, your contacts or your photos. We have no database with anything of yours in it, and we cannot identify you.

What the Game stores, and where

Your progress — levels, coins, the looks you have unlocked, your settings — is saved on your own device, in the app's private storage, with a backup copy next to it. It never leaves the device. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else. Deleting the Game deletes it.

The optional Performance check in Settings writes a small technical report (frame times, memory, device temperature state) on the device. It is sent nowhere unless you tap Share yourself and choose where it goes.

Advertising

The Game shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): a full-screen ad after every 8th game you finish, and videos you can choose to watch in exchange for something in the Game (double the coins of a win, a hint, free coins in the shop, or a second chance after a jam). There is no banner, and no ad ever appears in the middle of play.

To show and measure ads, Google's software in the Game may collect: your approximate location worked out from your IP address; device identifiers — the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and the identifier for vendors; information about how you interact with the Game and with ads; and crash and performance diagnostics. The advertising identifier is a number your phone gives out for advertising and nothing else; it is not your name, and you can reset it or switch it off in your phone's settings.

What Google is allowed to do with it depends on your answer, not on ours:

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) appears when you first open the Game, before any ad is requested. If you refuse, the ads you see are not personalised, or none are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency). iOS asks separately whether the Game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no — or never answer — the advertising identifier is not available to the Game or to Google. You can change the answer later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • SKAdNetwork. If you tap an ad and later install the advertised app, Apple's SKAdNetwork may tell the ad network that an install happened. That report comes from Apple, is delayed and aggregated, and does not identify you or your device.

Refusing changes nothing else about the Game: the same levels, the same coins, the same number of ads.

Google's own explanation of what it does with this data is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Game sells a monthly No Ads subscription (it removes the full-screen ads and gives the video rewards without watching) and packs of coins. We never see your card, your address or your name: the payment happens entirely inside the App Store, under the account you already have there. The Game only asks the App Store which of its purchases your account owns, and keeps the answer (product, transaction number, expiry date) on the device with the rest of your progress, so that a coin pack is credited exactly once and the subscription is recognised offline. None of it leaves the device.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and contains only abstract beads and strings. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from a child — the Game asks nobody for any personal detail at all. A parent who would rather their child saw no advertising can buy the No Ads subscription (the full-screen ads stop) or turn off tracking in the phone's settings.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct or delete personal data exist wherever personal data does. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to give you a copy of or to delete on request. For the data Google's advertising software collects, use the choices above or Google's own tools. The data on your device is yours: uninstalling the Game removes it.

The store

Downloading the Game from the App Store is a transaction between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change will be announced in the store listing.

Contact

Политика конфиденциальности (русский)

Сама игра не собирает персональные данные. Прогресс, монеты, открытые образы и настройки хранятся только на вашем устройстве; при удалении приложения они удаляются. У нас нет сервера и аккаунтов.

Реклама Google AdMob: полноэкранная реклама после каждой 8-й законченной игры и видео по вашему выбору; баннеров нет. Чтобы показывать и измерять рекламу, SDK Google может собирать приблизительное местоположение по IP-адресу, идентификаторы устройства (рекламный идентификатор — только если вы разрешили отслеживание), сведения о взаимодействии с игрой и рекламой, диагностику сбоев и производительности.

Где этого требует закон (ЕЭЗ, Великобритания, Швейцария, некоторые штаты США), игра до любого запроса рекламы показывает форму согласия Google; изменить выбор можно в «Настройки → Настройки рекламы и данных». iOS отдельно спрашивает разрешение на отслеживание (App Tracking Transparency). Отчёт Apple SKAdNetwork об установке после нажатия на рекламу не идентифицирует вас.

Покупки (наборы монет и подписка «Без рекламы») обрабатывает Apple; платёжные данные мы не получаем. Вопросы: shubina.sofia07@icloud.com.
