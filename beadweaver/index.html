Kinetic Bead Weaver

Support

Unweave the beads, top first.

Write to shubina.sofia07@icloud.com — say which iPhone and iOS version you use and the level number, and, if it helps, attach a screenshot. We answer every message.

How to play

  • Tap a pin (the round knob at the end of a string) to release that string. Its beads fly into the tray of their colour.
  • A string lying under another string cannot be released yet — its beads would jam. Release the string on top first.
  • After a jam you get one free Undo per attempt. Once it is used, you can Watch & continue (one short video, once per attempt) or simply try again. Hint shows a string that is safe to release right now.

The game does not open, or is slow

Close it fully (swipe it away from the app switcher) and open it again. If that does not help, restart the phone. The game needs no internet to play — a weak connection only affects the ads and the shop.

My progress disappeared

Progress and coins are saved on the phone itself, automatically, after every move. They survive updates. They do not survive deleting the app, and they do not move to a new phone by themselves. Settings → Reset progress starts the levels over and keeps your coins and looks. If something you had finished shows as unfinished, write to us with the phone model — we want to know.

Ads, and turning them off

A full-screen ad plays after every 8th game you finish — a level won, or a jam you accept with “Try again” or “Levels” — when you leave the result screen. Never in the middle of play, and there is no banner. Everything else is a video you choose to watch: double the coins of a win, a hint, free coins in the shop (up to 5 a day) or “Watch & continue” after a jam. If no video is available at that moment, the game says so and you lose nothing.

No Ads is a monthly subscription that removes the full-screen ads and gives you the video rewards without watching. It is in the Shop, with its price and renewal terms next to it. To cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the game does not cancel it.

I paid and did not get it

Open the Shop (or Settings) and tap Restore purchases — that asks the App Store again what you own, which fixes almost every case, including a new phone. If it still does not appear, write to us with the receipt Apple emailed you and we will sort it out.

Refunds

Refunds go through Apple, not us: reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue them ourselves, but tell us what went wrong — if it was our fault we want to fix it.

Поддержка (русский)

Вопросы и ошибки: shubina.sofia07@icloud.com. Укажите модель iPhone, версию iOS и номер уровня.

Нажмите на фиксатор, чтобы отпустить нить: бусины улетят в лоток своего цвета. Нить, которая лежит под другой, отпускать рано — бусины застрянут; сначала отпускайте верхнюю. Одна бесплатная отмена за попытку, затем можно «Продолжить за видео» (раз за попытку). Прогресс и монеты хранятся только на телефоне.

Полноэкранная реклама — после каждой 8-й законченной игры (победа или принятый проигрыш), когда вы уходите с экрана результата; во время игры — никогда. Остальные видео — только по вашему выбору. Подписка «Без рекламы» убирает полноэкранную рекламу; отменить её можно в настройках iPhone → ваше имя → Подписки. Покупки восстанавливаются кнопкой «Восстановить покупки» (Магазин или Настройки).
