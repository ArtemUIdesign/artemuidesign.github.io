Support

Drop, merge, and keep the tower low.

Write to shubina.sofia07@icloud.com — say which phone you use and, if it helps, attach a screenshot. We answer every message.

The game does not open, or is slow

Close it fully (swipe it away from the app switcher) and open it again. If that does not help, restart the phone. The Game needs no internet to play — a weak connection only affects the ads and the shop.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on the phone itself, automatically. It survives updates. It does not survive deleting the app, and it does not move to a new phone by itself. If something you had finished shows as unfinished, write to us with the phone model — we want to know.

Ads, and turning them off

There is no advertising at all for your first 20 games, and the things that normally cost you a video are simply free during that time. After it: a full-screen ad every 10th game you finish, and never in your first 20 games, and the videos you choose to watch (extra coins and a second chance). There is no banner anywhere in the Game, and there is never an ad in the middle of play.

No ads is a monthly subscription that removes the full-screen advertising. It is in the shop, with its price and renewal terms next to it. To cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the game does not cancel it.

I paid and did not get it

Open the shop and use Restore purchase — that asks the App Store again what you own, which fixes almost every case, including a new phone. If it still does not appear, write to us with the receipt Apple emailed you and we will sort it out.

Refunds

Refunds go through Apple, not us: reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue them ourselves, but tell us what went wrong — if it was our fault we want to fix it.
