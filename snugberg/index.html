A penguin colony,
45 islands of ice

Snugberg is a calm puzzle adventure. Sail from island to island, meet a hero and a new rule on each one, and watch your rookery grow with every board you solve.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone. Free to play, with optional purchases and ads. Plays offline.

Three ways to play the ice

One world, one colony and one save. Play all three kinds in turn, or follow only the one you love.

From level 1

Floes

Split the ice so that every penguin gets a floe exactly as big as its number. Every tile belongs to one floe.

From island 2

Polar Night

Place lamp penguins until every tile of ice is lit. No two lamps may see each other, and numbers count the lamps next to them.

From island 4

Harbour

Tap the boats in the right order so they sail out of the frozen bay, and the penguins of each colour climb aboard.

What is inside

  • 45 islands and the open sea beyond them: over 27,000 boards
  • Every board has exactly one solution, reachable by logic alone
  • A new rule on every island, shown on its hero's card
  • 100 extra levels on every island, whenever you want to stay
  • Three fresh boards every day and a monthly ice sculpture
  • An endless run against a friendly clock
  • 46 penguin heroes and a rookery that grows as you play
  • Undo, Redo and Sonar hints whenever you get stuck
  • No account and no internet needed to play
  • English, Español, Deutsch, Français, Русский

Support

Answers to the questions we get most often. Anything else: write to us, we answer every message.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Write to shubina.sofia07@icloud.com. Tell us which iPhone you use and, if it helps, attach a screenshot. You can also open Settings → Help → Support in the game.

The game does not open, or feels slow

Close it fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again. If that does not help, restart the iPhone. Snugberg needs no internet to play; a weak connection only affects ads and the shop's prices.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, automatically, after every move. It stays through updates. It is removed if you delete the app, and it does not move to another phone by itself. There is no account and no server, so we cannot restore it for you.

When do ads appear, and how do I turn them off?

Full-screen ads appear only between games, never during play: none before level 17, and after that at most one ad every 20 finished games (levels, daily boards and endless runs all count, won or lost).

Videos are always your choice: a free Sonar hint, a doubled catch, or a second chance. Remove ads in the Shop is a one-time purchase that turns off the full-screen ads for good; the videos you choose to watch stay available.

I paid but did not get my purchase

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom (it is also in Settings). This asks the App Store again what you own and brings back Remove ads, including on a new iPhone. Hints and krill are added the moment you buy them; if something is still missing, write to us with the receipt Apple emailed you.

Refunds

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but please tell us what went wrong.

Privacy and ad choices

Where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads; you can change your choices later in Settings → Ads & privacy → Ad settings. Tracking permission lives in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details: Privacy Policy.

How do I change the language?

In the game: Settings → Display & language. Snugberg speaks English, Spanish, German, French and Russian.
