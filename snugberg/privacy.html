Privacy Policy

Snugberg: Penguin Islands for iPhone · effective 10 November 2026

The short version

  • You never need an account. Your progress, settings and purchases record stay on your iPhone; we have no server and receive none of it.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your name, card or Apple ID.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it you are asked for consent first, and Apple asks whether Google may use your advertising identifier.
  • You can change your ad choices at any time, or buy Remove ads to turn off the full-screen ads.

1. Who we are

Snugberg: Penguin Islands (“Snugberg”, “the game”) is made and published by Sofia Aleksandra Subina, an independent developer in Estonia (“we”, “us”). For anything about this policy or your data, write to shubina.sofia07@icloud.com.

2. What the game keeps on your iPhone

To let you continue where you stopped, the game saves on your device: which boards you have solved and the board you are playing, your rookery, heroes, krill, hints and pebbles, your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, colour help), daily-puzzle progress, how many games you have finished since the last ad, and the identifiers of App Store transactions already delivered (so that nothing is given twice).

This data is stored only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own backups, if you make them). It is never sent to us. We do not run analytics of our own, have no server and no accounts. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The game receives from the App Store only a signed record of what was bought, which it checks on the device to deliver your item. We do not receive your name, email address, payment details or Apple ID.

4. Advertising by Google AdMob

Snugberg is free to play and is funded by ads shown through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC. The SDK runs inside the game and may collect the following data to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them, prevent fraud and improve Google's services:

Google uses this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and tracking

  • Consent form. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland (and wherever else Google's consent rules apply), the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes. If you refuse everything, the game asks for no tracking permission and shows only ads that do not use personal data, where available.
  • Apple's tracking prompt. On the first launch the game may show Apple's “Allow to track” question. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, Google does not receive the advertising identifier and ads are not personalised with it. The game plays exactly the same either way.

Your ad choices

  • In the game: Settings → Ads & privacy → Ad settings reopens the consent form where it applies; otherwise Tracking there opens the iPhone's settings for the game.
  • On the iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Report an ad in the game's settings lets you tell us about an ad that looks wrong.
  • The one-time Remove ads purchase turns off all full-screen ads. Videos you choose to watch stay available.

5. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Keeping your progress on your device and delivering purchases is necessary to provide the game you asked for. Personalised advertising and the use of your advertising identifier rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Non-personalised advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention by Google rely on legitimate interests in funding a free game and keeping ads safe.

6. Children

Snugberg is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data has reached us, write to us and we will help.

7. Keeping, sharing and moving data

We keep no personal data about you, so we have nothing to sell or share. Data collected by Google is kept and may be processed outside your country under Google's policies and safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability and to withdraw consent. As we hold no personal data about you, data on your iPhone is under your control (deleting the app deletes it), and requests about advertising data go to Google, for example through Google's privacy tools. We are happy to help with any request: shubina.sofia07@icloud.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority; in Estonia this is the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon.

9. Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
