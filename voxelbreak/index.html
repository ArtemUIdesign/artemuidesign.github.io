Voxel Break

Support

Pull the figure apart, magnet by magnet.

Write to shubina.sofia07@icloud.com — say which phone you use, which level you are on and, if it helps, attach a screenshot. We answer every message.

How to play

Tap a cube to fire the magnet in the cannon. A magnet pulls the whole connected group of cubes of its own colour that have the opposite pole (+ and −). Drag to turn the figure and reach the cubes at the back. You can load one of the magnets waiting in NEXT instead (on the early levels), or reload one from storage.

A magnet that matches nothing goes into storage. Storage has four sockets: when all four are full, the figure falls apart and the try is lost. From level 13 some groups are frozen: they cannot be pulled until you pull a group that touches the ice.

Stuck? Hint finds a move from exactly where you are (free in the lessons, then a few per try). Undo takes back your last shot once per try on most levels. Every one of the 500 levels has been solved with the same rules before it was shipped.

The game does not open, or is slow

Close it fully (swipe it away from the app switcher) and open it again. If that does not help, restart the phone. The game needs no internet to play. The biggest figures have more than two thousand cubes; if a level feels slow on your phone, tell us the phone model — we want to know.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on the phone itself, automatically, after every level. It survives updates. It does not survive deleting the app, and it does not move to a new phone by itself. If something you had finished shows as unfinished, write to us with the phone model.

Want to jump ahead? Settings → Unlock all levels lets you play any level in any order.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • a full-screen ad after every third finished game — a win, a loss, or a level you leave with Restart or Home after at least three shots. It plays when you leave the result screen, never in the middle of a level;
  • after a loss you can choose to watch a video to continue: your storage empties and you keep the figure. Once per try. If no video is available right now, the button says so — tap it again later, or simply try the level again.

There is no banner, and the game sells nothing. Without an internet connection no ads are shown and the game plays as usual.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland Google asks for your consent first; where Google requires it, Settings has a Privacy options button to change your answer. How the game handles your data: Privacy Policy.

Sound, vibration and motion

Music, sound effects and vibration each have their own switch in Settings. If fast movement bothers you, turn on Reduce motion in the game's Settings (the game also follows Reduce Motion in iOS).
