Voxel Break

Privacy Policy

Pull the figure apart, magnet by magnet.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how Sofia Aleksandra Subina (“we”, “us”) handles information in the iPhone game Voxel Break (on the App Store as “Magnet Merge: Voxel Break”; the “Game”). The short version: the Game itself collects nothing about you and we hold no data of yours anywhere. The Game is free and shows advertising from Google AdMob — which is what this policy is mostly about. Its only purchase is the optional No Ads subscription, which is handled entirely by Apple.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no server of its own. It does not ask for your name, your email, your location, your contacts or your photos. We have no database with anything of yours in it, and we cannot identify you.

What the Game stores, and where

Your progress (completed levels and their stars), how many games you have finished since the last ad, and your settings (music, sound effects, vibration, reduce motion, unlock all levels), how many minutes you have played in your first 10 minutes, and whether No Ads is on are saved on your iPhone, in the Game's own storage, with a backup copy in the Game's preferences on the same device. They never leave the device; we cannot see them, and neither can anyone else. Deleting the Game deletes them, and Settings → Reset progress in the Game erases your progress at any time.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

For the first 10 minutes of play after you install the Game there are no ads at all (and nothing is offered for sale). After that the Game shows a full-screen ad after every third finished game — a win, a loss, or a level left with Restart or Home after at least three shots — when you leave the result screen, never in the middle of a level. After a loss you can choose to watch a rewarded video to continue. There is no banner. Without an internet connection no ads are shown and the Game plays as usual.

The ads come from Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The consent and tracking questions below appear shortly after you first open the Game, before any ad is requested. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may receive: your IP address (from which it estimates an approximate location); a device identifier — the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, otherwise identifiers limited to this app or developer; advertising data such as which ads were shown; your interactions with them (taps, video views); and performance and crash data of the SDK. Google uses this to show and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same one and to prevent fraud, and may share it with the companies whose ads it shows. Google's own rules apply: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

SKAdNetwork. The Game lists the SKAdNetwork identifiers of Google and its partners, as Google requires. With SKAdNetwork (AdAttributionKit), iOS itself may later tell an ad network that an ad shown in the Game led to an app being installed. Apple decides what is sent and when; it does not include your identity or the advertising identifier.

Your choices

  • Consent (Google UMP). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before any ad is requested. What Google may do then follows your answer: if you do not agree to ads being requested, the Game requests none (and a video to continue is simply not available); if you refuse personalisation, the ads are not personalised. Where Google requires it, you can change your answer at any time in the Game: Settings → Privacy options.
  • Tracking (Apple ATT). iOS asks whether the Game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. The advertising identifier is shared only if you allow it; if you say no, the Game asks Google for non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Refusing changes nothing else about the Game: the same levels and the same number of ads.

Purchases

The Game offers one optional in-app purchase, the No Ads auto-renewable subscription (US$7.99 a month, or the local equivalent shown in the Game), which removes the full-screen ads between games; the optional rewarded videos stay available. It is offered only after your first 10 minutes of play. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple under your Apple ID; we receive no payment details and no personal information from Apple. The Game only asks Apple, on your device, whether the subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. There is no in-game currency.

Other third parties

Apart from Google's advertising and consent software, the Game contains no third-party service. It is built with open-source libraries (Capacitor, three.js) and fonts that run entirely on your device and send nothing anywhere. If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics; Apple collects them under its own privacy policy, not the Game.

Links

The Privacy policy, Terms of use and Support links in the Game's Settings open this website in Safari. Your browser and this site's host (GitHub Pages) handle those visits under their own policies.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the App Store's Kids category. We do not knowingly collect anything from a child, and the Game asks nobody for any personal detail at all.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct or delete personal data exist wherever personal data does. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to copy or delete on request; the data on your iPhone is yours and uninstalling the Game removes it. For data Google received through ads, use the choices above or Google's own tools.

The store

Downloading the Game from the App Store is a transaction between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, this page is updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes with it.
