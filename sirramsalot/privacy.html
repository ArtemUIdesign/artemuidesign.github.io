Sir Ramsalot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Sir Ramsalot is published by Sofia Aleksandra Subina.

The short version. Sir Ramsalot has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your progress, your garage, your coins, gems and crates are stored on your own device and never sent to us. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising service and Apple's payment service need in order to work.

What we store, and where

Your progress (stages cleared, stars, the parts you own and their upgrade levels, coins, gems, crates, boosts, missions, the Season Road, your purchases and your settings) is written to your device's local storage by the app itself. It stays on the device. We have no copy of it, no way to read it and no account system that could link it to you. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Sir Ramsalot shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, and only in one form: short videos that you choose to watch in exchange for a reward (double coins, a revive, a free crate, gems and the like). There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt the game. During your first ten minutes in the game there are no ads at all.

To serve these videos, the AdMob software in the app communicates with Google and may collect a device advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your IP address, device and network information, diagnostic data and data about the ads you were shown and how you interacted with them. Google uses this to select, measure and improve ads. We do not receive that data and cannot identify you from it.

On iOS the app asks for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before the advertising identifier can be used for personalised advertising. If you decline, the videos still work and still give the same rewards; they are just not personalised. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears first, and where the law requires it you can reopen your choices with Privacy choices at the bottom of the in-game shop.

Google's own policy explains what they collect and why: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchases

Gems, coins, crates, bundles, the Starter Pack, the Growth Fund, the Month Card, the Season Road Premium passes and the VIP Garage subscription can be bought with real money through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app is only told whether a purchase succeeded, so it can deliver what you bought, and it can ask the App Store which permanent items and subscriptions your Apple ID owns when you tap Restore purchases. None of this is offered during your first ten minutes in the game.

Children

Sir Ramsalot is a cartoon car-combat game with no chat, no user-generated content and no social features. It shows optional advertising and offers purchases, and it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on request. Deleting the app removes everything the game stored. For data held by Google in connection with advertising, use the Google link above or contact Google directly. If you have a question about any of this, write to shubina.sofia07@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date at the top.
