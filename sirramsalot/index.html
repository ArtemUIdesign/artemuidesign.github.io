How to play

Tap PLAY in the lobby to start a run. Drag anywhere on the screen to steer; your truck's guns fire by themselves. Ram enemy cars, collect upgrades as you level up, survive until the boss arrives and wreck it to clear the stage. Coins, gems and crates you earn upgrade your truck in the garage.

Ads

Sir Ramsalot shows no banner and no ads between stages. The only ads are optional reward videos that start when you tap a button (for example double coins, a free crate or the one revive per run). If a video is not available, nothing is lost — try again a little later. Your first 10 minutes of play have no ads and no purchase offers at all.

Purchases and VIP

All purchases are optional and go through Apple. VIP Garage is an auto-renewable monthly subscription: payment is charged to your Apple ID, it renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period, and you can manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings. To get your purchases back on a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of any shop tab.

My progress

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. The game has no accounts, so deleting the game deletes your progress.
