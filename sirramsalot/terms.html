Purchases

Some things in the game can be bought with real money through the App Store: gem packs, coin packs, the Starter Pack, the Power-Up, Daily and World packs, breaking the Piggy Bank, the Growth Fund, the Month Card, Season Road Premium and Premium+, crate, boost, Tune Kit and paint bundles, and the VIP Garage subscription. None of them is offered during your first ten minutes in the game.

Gems, coins, crates, boosts and Tune Kits are consumable: once spent or opened they are gone. The Month Card pays its daily gift for thirty days from the day it was bought; Season Road Premium and Premium+ last until the end of the calendar month (the season) in which they were bought. Neither is restored on another device. The Starter Pack and the Growth Fund are permanent for the Apple ID that bought them and can be brought back with Restore purchases in the shop.

Crates give random rewards. The chance of every reward in every crate, and the guarantee counters, are shown in the game (the ODDS button) before anything is opened or bought, and those shown chances are the ones the game uses.

Coins, gems and other in-game items are not money. They have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money or transferred to another person, and exist only inside your copy of the game.

All payments are handled by Apple. Refunds are governed by Apple's policy and are requested from Apple, not from us: Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

The VIP Garage subscription

VIP Garage is an auto-renewing monthly subscription ($7.99 per month in the U.S.; the price in your currency is shown in the app before you buy). While it is active it gives +10% coins from every run, a larger and faster garage income, a second free crate in the daily gift, and bonus rewards without watching a video (the revive still asks for a video).

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically each month unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage the subscription and turn off auto-renew in your Apple ID account settings (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions) after purchase. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the period you already paid for runs to its end. If the subscription lapses, the VIP perks stop and nothing else about your game changes.
